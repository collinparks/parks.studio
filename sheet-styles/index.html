Find your place in a big spreadsheet

Sheet Styles brings Google Docs headings to Google Sheets. Mark a cell as a Title or Heading, and it shows up in a clickable outline that jumps you straight there.

Install for Google Sheets See pricing

Free for up to 10 headings per spreadsheet. Pro is $20 a year.

Three steps from scrolling to jumping

  1. Install the add-on

    Add Sheet Styles from the Google Workspace Marketplace. It shows up under Extensions in every spreadsheet you open.

  2. Mark your headings

    Select a cell and click Title, H1, H2 or H3. Your formatting stays exactly as it is, or apply a ready-made heading style if you want one.

  3. Jump anywhere

    Open the outline and click any heading to go straight to it. Search the list when it gets long.

Headings that stay where you put them

  • Anchored to the cellInsert rows, delete columns, restyle the text. The heading moves with its cell and stays in the outline.
  • Ranked or in orderSee every Title first, then Heading 1s, 2s and 3s, or switch to the order they appear in the sheet.
  • The whole spreadsheet at onceWith Pro, one outline lists headings from every tab, grouped by tab name.
  • Styles like Google DocsTitle, Subtitle, Heading 1 to 3, Table header and Note. Edit them, add your own, or copy a style from any cell.

Help

How do I unlock Pro after buying?

Your Gumroad receipt includes a license key. In any spreadsheet, open Extensions > Sheet Styles > Open outline and styles, click Upgrade, paste the key and click Activate. Pro then works in every spreadsheet on that Google account.

Does marking a heading change how my sheet looks?

No. The Title, H1, H2 and H3 buttons in the outline only add the cell to the outline. Your fonts, colors and sizes stay exactly as they are. The Styles tab is there if you do want to restyle a cell.

What can Sheet Styles see in my Google account?

Only the spreadsheet you have open, and only while you use the add-on. It can't open your other files, your email or your Drive. Details are in the privacy policy.

I moved a heading and it left the outline. Why?

Headings follow their cell when you insert or delete rows and columns. If you cut a heading and paste it somewhere else, the mark stays behind on the old cell. Unmark the old cell and mark the new one.

Can my coworkers see the outline?

The headings are saved inside the spreadsheet, so anyone who installs Sheet Styles sees the same outline. People without the add-on just see your sheet as usual.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Pro stays on until the end of the year you paid for, then the add-on returns to the free plan. Every heading you already marked stays in the outline. You just can't add more past 10 per spreadsheet, and the All tabs view turns off.
