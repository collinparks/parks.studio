Sheet Styles

Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Sheet Styles is a Google Sheets add-on made by Parks Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the add-on can access, what it stores, and what it shares. The short version: it works only inside the spreadsheet you have open, it does not collect analytics, and it never sells or shares your data.

What the add-on can access

When you install Sheet Styles, Google asks you to allow these permissions:

  • See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Sheets spreadsheets you use this add-on with. Sheet Styles reads and changes cell formatting when you apply a style, reads the text of cells you mark as headings so it can list them in the outline, and stores heading marks inside the spreadsheet. It cannot open any other file in your Google Drive.
  • Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars inside Google applications. This shows the Sheet Styles sidebar.
  • Connect to an external service. This is used only to check a Pro license key with Gumroad, as described below.

What is stored, and where

  • Heading marks are saved as hidden metadata inside the spreadsheet itself, attached to the row and column of each heading. They are visible to the add-on for anyone with access to that spreadsheet.
  • Style settings for a spreadsheet are saved inside that spreadsheet.
  • Your default style set and your license details (your license key, the purchase email Gumroad returns, and when the license was last checked) are saved in your own Google account's add-on storage, which only the add-on can read for your account.

All of this stays within Google's systems. We run no servers and no database of our own, and we cannot see your spreadsheets.

What is shared

If you activate Pro, the add-on sends your license key and our product ID to Gumroad's license API (api.gumroad.com) to confirm the purchase is valid. It does this when you activate and then at most once a day. No spreadsheet content is ever sent. Gumroad processes your payment and purchase details under its own privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

Sheet Styles' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Spreadsheet data is used only to provide the add-on's features to you, and is never transferred to anyone or used to train AI models.

Removing your data

  • To remove heading marks from a spreadsheet, unmark the headings in the outline, or delete the spreadsheet.
  • To remove your license details, click Upgrade > Remove license from this account in the sidebar.
  • To remove the add-on and its access, uninstall it from Extensions > Add-ons > Manage add-ons, and remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Sheet Styles is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will be noted on the Sheet Styles page.
